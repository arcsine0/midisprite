# Sample credits

Melodic samples come from **FluidR3_GM**, by Frank Wen et al., distributed under [Creative Commons Attribution 3.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/). The MIDI.js Soundfonts export is maintained at <https://github.com/gleitz/midi-js-soundfonts>.

The 47 General MIDI drum samples were extracted from the MIDI.js export at <https://github.com/dave4mpls/midi-js-soundfonts-with-drums>, which adds the drum map to the same FluidR3 bank.

This project bundles nine melodic pitch roots per instrument and the available drum notes as MP3 files. Tone.Sampler transposes the melodic roots to cover pitches between them. The source MP3 samples are mixed offline and encoded as 44.1 kHz mono, 16-bit PCM WAV for preview.
